What does Quorum+ include?

Quorum is free: every day you get 9 global questions and 3 about your continent, each with context and the best argument for each side, your prediction, your vote and the global results, plus automatic translation and the daily reminder.

Quorum+ is an optional monthly subscription that adds:

  • How every continent voted on each question, and the crowd’s average prediction
  • Your full archive, calibration and stats
  • A daily recap of the question that split the world

New subscribers can try it free for 7 days. After that it is US$0.99 a month in the United States or R$4,90 in Brazil; the store shows the price in your currency before you confirm. You can subscribe from the Quorum+ row on the Today screen, from Settings → Quorum+, or from the locked By region card in a question’s results.

How do I cancel Quorum+?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you subscribed with:

  • iPhone: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, choose Quorum+ and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from Quorum: Settings → Manage subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose Quorum and tap Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next renewal. Quorum+ stays active until the end of the period you already paid for (or of the free trial). Deleting the app or your Quorum account does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore Quorum+?

In Quorum, go to Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you subscribed with. Subscriptions don’t move between platforms: one started on iPhone can’t be restored on Android, and vice versa.

I subscribed, but Quorum+ still looks locked

Check your internet connection and tap Settings → Restore purchases. If the payment is pending (for example, waiting for a parent’s approval or for a payment method to clear), Quorum+ unlocks as soon as the store confirms it. Still stuck? Write to us.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store where you subscribed, under its own rules; we can’t issue them directly. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play refunds. To stop future charges, also cancel the subscription (see above).

How do I change my region?

Tap the region name at the top of the Today screen, or go to Settings → Region. Regions are continents: South America, North America (including Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean), Europe, Africa, Asia and Oceania.

Your first region is suggested from your phone’s region setting; Quorum never uses your location. Every day you get global questions plus a few about your continent, all in the same list.

Which languages does Quorum support?

The app is available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Japanese, and follows your phone’s language. To change the language for Quorum only: on iPhone, Settings → Apps → Quorum → Language (shown when your phone has more than one preferred language); on Android 13 or later, Settings → System → Languages → App languages (names may vary by manufacturer).

How does automatic translation work?

When a question isn’t available in your phone’s language, Quorum translates it on your device and marks it Translated automatically. Tap Show original to see the original text. The first time, your phone may need to download a language pack. If translation isn’t available, the question is shown in the closest available language.

You can turn this off in Settings → Translate questions automatically.

How do I set the daily reminder?

Go to Settings → Daily reminder, choose a time and allow notifications when asked. If reminders don’t arrive, check that notifications for Quorum are allowed in your phone’s settings.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Delete account → Delete permanently. This deletes your anonymous account and, if you subscribed, the Quorum+ subscription record on our server. Votes already counted stay in the totals, with no link to you.

Deleting your account does not cancel Quorum+. Cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions (iPhone) or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions (Android). If you keep it, use Restore purchases to reconnect it.

If you no longer have the app: install it again; if you subscribed to Quorum+, tap Restore purchases; then delete the account. Or write to us; for a Google Play subscription, include the order number (it starts with “GPA.”). Anonymous accounts with no Quorum+ subscription are deleted automatically after 90 days without use.

How are the questions made? Is Quorum neutral?

Every day the developer prepares the questions with the help of AI tools, using recent news when relevant. An automated check screens each question for neutrality before it is published, and the developer spot-checks what is published. Questions are about open issues where reasonable people disagree, and each one shows the best argument for each side.

We don’t ask about elections or candidates, don’t accuse named people, don’t give medical, legal or financial advice, and don’t target any group. If a question seems unfair, open it and tap Report question. Every report is reviewed.

Where do the news summaries come from?

The Today screen shows short summaries of recent news. They are written with AI from reports published by third parties and go through the same automated neutrality check as the questions. A summary may contain errors and is not the publisher’s own words: tap it to read the original report, which is always the reference.

Company names and logos shown with the news are trademarks of their respective owners. Quorum is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. If a summary seems wrong, write to us.

Why don’t I see results for my region?

Results by continent are part of Quorum+: after you vote, the By region card shows how each continent answered. To protect privacy, a continent appears only after enough people from it have answered the question. The global total is shown to everyone after they vote.

Is Quorum a scientific poll?

No. Results only reflect the people who chose to answer in the app, so they are not representative of any population.

Como as perguntas são feitas? O Quorum é neutro?

Todo dia o desenvolvedor prepara as perguntas com ajuda de ferramentas de IA, usando notícias recentes quando faz sentido. Uma verificação automática avalia a neutralidade de cada pergunta antes da publicação, e o desenvolvedor confere por amostragem o que é publicado. As perguntas tratam de temas em aberto, em que pessoas razoáveis discordam, e cada uma mostra o melhor argumento de cada lado.

Não perguntamos sobre eleições ou candidatos, não acusamos pessoas pelo nome, não damos conselhos médicos, jurídicos ou financeiros e não miramos nenhum grupo. Se uma pergunta parecer injusta, abra-a e toque em Reportar pergunta. Todas as denúncias são analisadas.

De onde vêm os resumos de notícias?

A tela Hoje mostra resumos curtos de notícias recentes. Eles são escritos com IA a partir de reportagens publicadas por terceiros e passam pela mesma verificação automática de neutralidade das perguntas. Um resumo pode conter erros e não reproduz as palavras do veículo: toque nele para ler a reportagem original, que é sempre a referência.

Os nomes e logos de empresas exibidos com as notícias são marcas de seus respectivos titulares. O Quorum não é afiliado a eles nem endossado por eles. Se um resumo parecer errado, escreva para nós.

Por que não vejo os resultados da minha região?

Os resultados por continente fazem parte do Quorum+: depois que você vota, o cartão Por região mostra como cada continente respondeu. Para proteger a privacidade, um continente só aparece depois que pessoas suficientes de lá responderam à pergunta. O total global aparece para todo mundo depois de votar.

O Quorum é uma pesquisa científica?

Não. Os resultados refletem apenas as pessoas que escolheram responder no app, então não representam nenhuma população.

Si vous n’avez plus l’app : réinstallez-la ; si vous êtes abonné à Quorum+, touchez Restaurer les achats ; puis supprimez le compte. Ou écrivez-nous ; pour un abonnement Google Play, indiquez le numéro de commande (il commence par « GPA. »). Les comptes anonymes sans abonnement Quorum+ sont supprimés automatiquement après 90 jours sans utilisation.

Plus de détails dans la Politique de confidentialité.

Comment les questions sont-elles faites ? Quorum est-il neutre ?

Chaque jour, le développeur prépare les questions avec l’aide d’outils d’IA, en s’appuyant sur l’actualité récente quand c’est pertinent. Un contrôle automatique vérifie la neutralité de chaque question avant sa publication, et le développeur contrôle par sondage ce qui est publié. Les questions portent sur des sujets ouverts, sur lesquels des personnes raisonnables ne sont pas d’accord, et chacune présente le meilleur argument de chaque camp.

Nous ne posons pas de questions sur les élections ou les candidats, n’accusons pas de personnes nommément, ne donnons pas de conseils médicaux, juridiques ou financiers et ne visons aucun groupe. Si une question vous semble injuste, ouvrez-la et touchez Signaler la question. Chaque signalement est examiné.

D’où viennent les résumés d’actualités ?

L’écran Aujourd’hui affiche de courts résumés de l’actualité récente. Ils sont rédigés avec l’IA à partir d’articles publiés par des tiers et passent par le même contrôle automatique de neutralité que les questions. Un résumé peut contenir des erreurs et ne reprend pas les mots de l’éditeur : touchez-le pour lire l’article original, qui fait toujours foi.

Les noms et logos d’entreprises affichés avec les actualités sont des marques de leurs titulaires respectifs. Quorum n’est ni affilié à eux ni approuvé par eux. Si un résumé vous semble erroné, écrivez-nous.

Pourquoi je ne vois pas les résultats de ma région ?

Les résultats par continent font partie de Quorum+ : après votre vote, la carte Par région montre comment chaque continent a répondu. Pour protéger la vie privée, un continent n’apparaît qu’une fois que suffisamment de personnes de ce continent ont répondu à la question. Le total mondial s’affiche pour tout le monde après le vote.

Quorum est-il un sondage scientifique ?

Non. Les résultats ne reflètent que les personnes qui ont choisi de répondre dans l’app ; ils ne sont donc représentatifs d’aucune population.

Wie entstehen die Fragen? Ist Quorum neutral?

Jeden Tag erstellt der Entwickler die Fragen mithilfe von KI-Werkzeugen, bei Bedarf auf Basis aktueller Nachrichten. Eine automatische Prüfung bewertet jede Frage vor der Veröffentlichung auf Neutralität, und der Entwickler kontrolliert Veröffentlichtes stichprobenartig. Die Fragen behandeln offene Themen, bei denen vernünftige Menschen unterschiedlicher Meinung sind, und jede zeigt das stärkste Argument jeder Seite.

Wir fragen nicht nach Wahlen oder Kandidaten, beschuldigen keine namentlich genannten Personen, geben keine medizinischen, rechtlichen oder finanziellen Ratschläge und richten uns gegen keine Gruppe. Wenn dir eine Frage unfair erscheint, öffne sie und tippe auf Frage melden. Jede Meldung wird geprüft.

Woher kommen die Nachrichten-Zusammenfassungen?

Der Bildschirm „Heute“ zeigt kurze Zusammenfassungen aktueller Nachrichten. Sie werden mit KI aus Berichten Dritter erstellt und durchlaufen dieselbe automatische Neutralitätsprüfung wie die Fragen. Eine Zusammenfassung kann Fehler enthalten und gibt nicht die Worte des Mediums wieder: Tippe darauf, um den Originalbericht zu lesen, der immer maßgeblich ist.

Die mit den Nachrichten gezeigten Firmennamen und Logos sind Marken ihrer jeweiligen Inhaber. Quorum ist mit ihnen nicht verbunden und wird nicht von ihnen unterstützt. Wenn dir eine Zusammenfassung falsch erscheint, schreib uns.

Warum sehe ich keine Ergebnisse für meine Region?

Ergebnisse nach Kontinent gehören zu Quorum+: Nach deiner Stimme zeigt die Karte Nach Region, wie jeder Kontinent geantwortet hat. Zum Schutz der Privatsphäre erscheint ein Kontinent erst, wenn genügend Menschen von dort die Frage beantwortet haben. Die globale Summe sieht jeder, nachdem er abgestimmt hat.

Ist Quorum eine wissenschaftliche Umfrage?

Nein. Die Ergebnisse spiegeln nur die Menschen wider, die in der App antworten wollten; sie sind also für keine Bevölkerung repräsentativ.

質問はどのように作られていますか？ Quorum は中立ですか？

開発者が毎日、AI ツールの助けを借り、必要に応じて最近のニュースをもとに質問を作成しています。公開前に自動チェックで各質問の中立性を確認し、公開された内容は開発者が抜き取りで確認しています。質問は、理性的な人の間でも意見が分かれる未解決のテーマを扱い、どの質問にも双方の最も強い論拠を示しています。

選挙や候補者についての質問、名指しで個人を非難する質問、医療・法律・金融に関する助言、特定の集団を標的にする質問は扱いません。不公平だと感じる質問があれば、その質問を開いて質問を報告をタップしてください。報告はすべて確認しています。

ニュースの要約はどこから来ていますか？

「今日」画面には、最近のニュースの短い要約が表示されます。要約は第三者が公開した報道をもとに AI で作成し、質問と同じ自動の中立性チェックを経ています。要約には誤りが含まれることがあり、報道機関自身の文章ではありません。タップすると元の記事を読めます。元の記事が常に正となります。

ニュースとともに表示される企業名やロゴは各所有者の商標です。Quorum はそれらの企業と提携しておらず、推奨も受けていません。要約に誤りがあると思われる場合は、ご連絡ください。

自分の地域の結果が表示されないのはなぜ？

大陸ごとの結果は Quorum+ の機能です。投票後、地域別カードに各大陸の回答が表示されます。プライバシー保護のため、大陸の結果はその大陸から十分な数の回答が集まってから表示されます。世界全体の集計は、投票後に誰でも見られます。

Quorum は科学的な世論調査ですか？

いいえ。結果はアプリで回答することを選んだ人だけを反映しているため、特定の集団を代表するものではありません。
